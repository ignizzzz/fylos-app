The building blocks are lovely, but the layout is the same centred card every onboarding uses: illustration in a circle, sparkles, centred text, a solid button. Nothing here says Fylos rather than any pet app. The after keeps the exact same content and turns it into an editorial spread.

After · four screens

Across the app

The same language scales: cards become paper plates, section titles get the serif and a painted arc, empty states get a spot illustration, and the paw-path becomes the progress and navigation motif. Warm light, on brand.

The in-app editor

Because every pet page is a plate, owners could pick the illustration, the frame and the headline for their Fylos. Personal, still unmistakably Fylos. A natural next surface once the language is set.
