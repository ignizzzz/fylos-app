Chapter 01

Health, remembered.

Every pet arrives with paperwork. Vaccine cards, vet letters, the folded receipt from the first visit. It scatters — a drawer here, a glovebox there — until the day you need it fast.

Fylos keeps it in one quiet place. Vaccines, meds and records — with you, not in a drawer. When a booster comes due, you hear about it early and gently, before it is urgent.

field note — the rabies card lived in the glovebox for a year. Never again.

Chapter 02

A gentle rhythm.

A good day with a dog has a shape. The early walk, the eight o’clock chew, the dinner that must never be late. None of it is hard — there is simply a lot of it.

Fylos lays the day out at a glance. Walks, meals, reminders — today, in order, settled with one tap. The rhythm stays yours. The remembering becomes ours.

field note: Bobby pulls left, always.

Chapter 03

People you trust.

Sooner or later, every pet parent needs another pair of hands. A walker on a long Tuesday. A sitter for the wedding weekend. A vet who already knows the story.

Fylos keeps them close. Walkers, sitters and vets — nearby, verified, booked in a few taps. You see who is coming, when, and how the walk went.

margin note — Marco brings his own treats. Bobby approves.
