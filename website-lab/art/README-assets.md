## Style — keep ALL of them the same family (match `public/onboarding/services.jpg`)
Delicate **watercolor + fine ink linework**, ONE centered subject, warm **vermilion–coral
pigment only** (~#E85D2A), soft translucent washes, gentle pigment granulation, thin
expressive hand-drawn ink lines, lots of negative space, on soft warm **cream** paper.
Elegant editorial minimalism, calm, premium. **Monochrome coral** — no other colours,
no black outlines, no text, plain background.

Reusable prompt (swap {SUBJECT}):
> delicate watercolor with fine ink linework, single centered subject: {SUBJECT}, warm
> vermilion-coral pigment (#E85D2A) only, soft translucent washes, gentle pigment
> granulation, thin expressive ink lines, generous negative space, soft cream paper,
> elegant editorial minimalism, calm premium, monochrome coral, no text, plain background
> `--ar 1:1 --style raw`

Consistency tip: reuse the **same style reference** for all of them (Midjourney: add
`--sref <link to one onboarding image, e.g. services>` on every prompt, or the same seed).
Generate them in one sitting so the brush/weight matches.

## Format
- Square **1:1**, **≥1500px** (2048 ideal), PNG or JPG.
- Subject centered, ~65–70% of the frame, **same scale** across all (they're a family).
- Background: plain cream/white is fine — I cut it to transparent. (Transparent PNG even better.)
