The fine print

Privacy, in plain words.

Last updated July 2026
This is the working draft for the preview site. Before public launch it goes past counsel for Swiss FADP review. Nothing below overstates what the site actually does.

Who we are

Fylos AG, Zürich, Switzerland, runs this website. For anything about your data, write to privacy@fylos.me.

What this website collects

Very little, on purpose.

  • Your pet’s name, if you type it. The film asks who you are strolling with. The name stays in your browser’s storage on your device so the story greets you next time. It is never transmitted to us.
  • Application forms. If you apply on /apply or /join, we receive what you send and use it only to read and reply to your application. Details sit next to each form. If your clinic or business is not a fit, applications are deleted within twelve months.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics today. If analytics ever arrive, they will stay off until you switch them on. Your choice is stored on your device.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete anything we hold about you, any time, at privacy@fylos.me. We answer personally.

Providers

To run this site and reach you we rely on a few trusted providers, some based outside Switzerland, always under proper safeguards. We never sell your data.
