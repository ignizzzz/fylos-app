How it works

From the first minute to the first walk.

Setup is usually a wall of empty fields. Fylos is a few warm minutes instead, and every step leaves you with something real and useful.

Free to start · about five minutes
1
Live now

Add your pet

A guided flow walks through the essentials: who they are, their breed, their personality, their daily care and their care team. Skip what you like now and come back later. It ends on a card with their name on it.

You will see: a “Welcome, Milo” card, assembled from what you entered.

2
Live now

Fill the health book

Add vaccines, medications, allergies and weight, plus his preferences and milestones. It is all yours to edit inline, and it stays in your pocket. The book holds what you and your vet put in it, so it is only ever as complete as you make it.

You will see: vaccine and weight tiles with status, and a tap-to-copy microchip number.

Explore the health book
3
Live now

Put his ID on the collar

Make Milo’s digital ID in the app, print it, and clip it on. If he ever wanders off, a neighbor scans it and reaches you in seconds, with no app to download. You always choose what a finder can see.

You will see: a QR ready to print, and a preview of the page a finder opens.

See how the ID works
4
Live now

Book care nearby

Pick a service, choose a real slot, say which pet it is for, add any extras, and see a clear total before a payment step. Then a calm “Request sent” screen tells you what happens next. Payments are secured by Stripe.

You will see: the slots, the running total, and a request confirmation.

See how booking works
5
Live nowLive map soon

Message and follow along

The moment the booking exists, chat opens. Send the heads-up, like “he’s jumpy near scooters”. During the walk your pro can share photo updates, and afterward you get a recap with his mood and a note. Following the walk live on a map is on the way.

You will see: a thread with your pro, photo updates, and an end-of-walk summary.

6
Live now

Keep the moments

The first beach day, the kibble that finally worked, the milestone you want to remember. The journal keeps them all, and the small notes ride along inside the book, so the next person who cares for Milo knows him a little better.

You will see: a date-grouped timeline you can add to, pin and search.

See the health book
