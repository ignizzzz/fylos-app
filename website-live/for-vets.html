How it works

One book, both sides of the leash

Nothing to install today. This is the shape of Fylos for vets when it goes live, so you know what you are joining.

A health book that is always current

Vaccines, next check, weight and allergies live in one place the owner keeps up. When they book you, that context comes with them, so the visit starts informed instead of starting from scratch.

Notes and prescriptions flow back

What you record after the visit returns to the same book, so the next person who cares for the pet is not guessing. It works both ways, and every step stays under the owner's consent.

The right people see the right part

The owner decides who sees what. You get the clinical history you need. A walker or sitter only sees the allergies and the routine. Every other pro sees only what the owner allows.

Fits how you already work

It links to the software you already use

When Fylos for vets goes live, it connects to your practice management system so a pet's record can sync both ways, with consent. On paper today? A simple Fylos web view works from day one. Here are the systems we are building toward first.
