The health book

Milo’s health book, always up to date.

A pet’s details usually live in your head and a few scattered notes. Fylos gathers them into one living book you carry everywhere, and share only on your terms.

The book
About Health Journal Documents Sharing

The book holds only what you and your vet put in it. It is not a medical service, and it does not give advice.

His health, at a glance

Vaccines, medications, allergies and weight, each with a plain status so nothing sneaks up on you. Mark a chew as taken, watch the weight trend, and keep the vet visits with their notes. Every value is one you or your vet enter, so the book is only ever as complete as you make it.

Every paper in one place

The passport, the insurance policy, the rabies certificate, the adoption papers. They usually live in email, a drawer and a photo roll, impossible to find when a border or a new vet asks. Keeping them together, organized by kind, is on the way.
