The fine print

Privacy, plainly.

Last updated July 16, 2026

Who we are

Fylos AG, Zürich, Switzerland, runs this website and the Fylos app. For anything about your data, write to privacy@fylos.me and a person will reply.

What this website collects

Nothing nonessential without your consent. Analytics and marketing cookies stay off until you accept them in the cookie banner, and you can change your mind any time from the Cookie preferences link in the footer. The site works fully if you decline.

Our analytics are built so that no name, email, message, or pet health detail is ever sent with an event, even after consent.

What the application forms collect

If you apply for early access, we receive what you type: your name, contact details, and what you tell us about your clinic or work. We use it only to assess and reply to your application, we never sell it, and if we are not a fit we delete applications within twelve months.

Your rights

Under Swiss data protection law you can ask to see, correct, or delete your details at any time at privacy@fylos.me.

This page is the plain language summary for the website and the early access period. The full privacy policy for the Fylos app ships with the app's public launch.
