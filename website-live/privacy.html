The fine print

Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: December 22, 2025

At FYLOS, we believe in radical transparency. This page explains exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it. Simple, honest, and straightforward.

What we collect

Your email address. We collect your email address when you join our waitlist. Partner application forms may also collect the business and contact details you submit.

Why we collect it

  • To notify you when we launch. We'll send you one email when FYLOS is ready. That's the entire purpose of the waitlist.
  • To review partner requests. If you apply as a FYLOS Pro partner, we use the submitted business details to review and follow up on your application.
  • To protect against spam. We use Google reCAPTCHA to help ensure real people submit our public forms, not bots.

What we don't do

  • No selling. We will never sell your personal information.
  • No marketing sharing. We don't share your email with third parties for marketing.
  • No spam. You'll only hear from us when there is a relevant FYLOS update or partner follow-up.

How we protect your data

  • Encrypted storage. Your data is stored in secure systems with industry-standard security practices.
  • Access control. Only authorized team members can access submitted data.
  • HTTPS. Data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols.

Third-party services

Google reCAPTCHA. We use Google reCAPTCHA v3 to protect our waitlist and public forms from spam and abuse. When reCAPTCHA runs, technical interaction data may be processed by Google on our behalf to help determine whether a request is likely to be from a real person. reCAPTCHA is used only for abuse prevention and form security.

Microsoft Application Insights. We use Microsoft Application Insights to understand whether the website and app are working correctly, measure page views, diagnose errors, and improve reliability. This may process technical data such as browser type, approximate region, request timing, page URL, and error details. We use this telemetry for diagnostics and service reliability, not for advertising.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data.
  • Delete your data.
  • Object to optional telemetry processing by choosing necessary-only cookies.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@fylos.me.

Questions?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, we're here to help. Write to privacy@fylos.me and a person will reply. Our Terms of Service explain the rules for using FYLOS.
