Why Fylos

Right now, your pet lives in a dozen places at once.

The vaccine card is in a drawer. The sitter is in your texts. The walker is on some other app. When someone asks what he’s allergic to, you are guessing. Fylos gathers all of it into one calm place, so the next person who cares for Milo already knows what he needs.

The health book that travels with him

One living profile for each pet: who he is, his vaccines, his medications, his allergies, his weight, his vet visits, and the small daily moments in a diary. You keep it, you edit it, and it is always in your pocket.

  • Allergies and routine, ready for any sitter
  • A diary of the walks, meals and milestones

An ID that gets him walked home

Make Milo’s digital ID in the app, print it, and put it on the collar. If he ever wanders off, a neighbor scans it and reaches you in seconds. No app for them to download, and you always choose what a finder sees.

  • One scan opens a simple “call his family” page
  • Private by default, your details stay yours
  • An engraved metal tag is coming soon

Book a walker or groomer, two doors down

The local pros people already trust, in one place. See their times and prices, request in two taps, and the moment the booking exists you can message them. Pay securely through Stripe, and cancel, reschedule or review when it is done.

  • Walks, sitting and grooming nearby
  • Message opens once the booking is made
  • Photos and a recap when the walk is done
