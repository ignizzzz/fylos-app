What the pilot is, and what it is not

What the pilot is

  • A careful first group. A handful of clinics and pros in Zurich and nearby, set up one by one with real help from us.
  • A conversation both ways. You get early access, we get honest feedback, and it genuinely changes what we build next.
  • Low friction to start. For clinics we confirm your registration ourselves through Switzerland's public registers, so there is nothing to upload now.

What it is not

  • Not a contract or a bill. Joining the pilot costs nothing and commits you to nothing. You can step out any time.
  • Not a finished product. Fylos for vets and the pro tools are still coming. You are helping shape them, not buying them.
  • Not a data grab. A pet's record only ever moves with the owner's consent, and going live for a clinic needs a separate data agreement.
