Rules for new surfaces

keep it on‑brand

Warm neutral canvas. Cream #F7F5F2 background, white surfaces, hairline dividers. The whole thing feels like paper and daylight.

Coral is the single accent. One warm coral for CTAs, active states, links and the icon fills. Never a rainbow of colors.

Peach chips + coral icons. Every list row / feature icon sits in a soft peach square with a coral Lucide glyph. Compact type.

Radius 16, warm shadows. Cards, buttons and avatars use a 16px radius; elevation is a soft warm shadow, with a coral glow reserved for the primary CTA.

Editorial serif for voice. Instrument Serif italic for the occasional headline moment; Inter / Inter Tight for everything structural.

No dark hero cards, no multi‑color tiles, no glossy gradient AI look. If it feels like a generic SaaS template, it's wrong.
